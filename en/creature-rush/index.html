About the game

Creature Rush is an arcade creature racing game for Android. The world is 3D; the creatures and the scenery are sprites.

The creature accelerates on its own. On a phone, you steer it by tilting the device: the neutral position is learned at the start of every race, so it does not matter how you hold the phone.

  • Played in landscape mode
  • Countdown, laps, timing, ranking and finish
  • A lap only counts when every checkpoint was taken in order
  • AI-driven creatures, with the same physics as the player's
  • Four stats per creature: top speed, acceleration, steering, grip
