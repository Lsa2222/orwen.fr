About the game

A 2D pixel-art platformer for Android, played in landscape.

The heroes do not fight: they run, jump, double jump and dodge through monster-filled lands to reach the safety of the camp.

  • Worlds: 3 biomes (Swamp, Snow City, Tiny Forest) with 5 levels each, and a boss chase at the end of each biome. You cannot attack the bosses: you escape them.
  • Stars: ★★★ for reaching the camp within the target time without losing a life.
  • Progression: a world map, a camp hub, and 3 heroes (1 at the start, 2 bought with the coins earned from stars).
  • Offline first: local save, plus an optional cloud save with Google Play Games.
  • Free to play: ads never appear during gameplay, rewarded videos are optional, and "Remove ads" and coin packs are sold through Google Play Billing.
  • Languages: English and French.
