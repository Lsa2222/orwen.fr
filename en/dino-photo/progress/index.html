Dino Photo

Dino Photo: progress

about 75%

Final polish

A rough percentage, deliberately cautious. As of October 9, 2026.

All eleven biomes and 21 species are in the game, in animated 3D, along with the photo mode, the missions and the encyclopedia. Work now focuses on smooth performance on phones and on the last few sounds.

This project is on hold for now.

Latest changes

  • The game now plays in French or English: the phone's language on first launch, then a choice in the settings (October 2026).
  • Gallery, photo mode and encyclopedia reworked: every photo keeps its image, the settings tuck away behind a button, and every species has its portrait (October 2026).
  • Photo spots reworked: an active volcano, a floating island, a shaft of light, a cliff, a ruined lighthouse, and a giant shell on the coast (October 2026).
  • Ground repainted in high definition across all eleven biomes, with brighter caves and lava cracks in the volcanic lands (October 2026).
  • All 21 species are now in animated 3D, flying and swimming included (October 2026).
  • New sounds: creature calls, footsteps, rain, ambiences and music (October 2026).

In progress

  • Smoothness tests on a mid-range phone, where the game does not yet run smoothly enough.
  • Last sounds being approved: the photographer's footsteps, thunder, running water, wingbeats, birds taking flight.
  • A final look at the scenery in all eleven biomes.
  • Touch joystick: forward and backward put the right way round, to be checked on a phone.

Coming next

  • Find what slows the game down on phones and fix it, with no change to the picture.
  • Picture quality adjusted automatically on modest phones, without taking anything away from the more powerful ones.
  • Release version to be put together and tested.
  • Store listing: text, screenshots, icon, rating.
