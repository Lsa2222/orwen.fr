Filmoigraphie: progress

about 40%

In development

A rough percentage, deliberately cautious. As of October 9, 2026.

A first version exists, for the web and for Android: artist pages with progress, appreciation levels, badges, search and friends. It dates from September 2026 and has not been changed since.

This project is on hold for now.

Latest changes

  • First version that can be installed on Android (September 2026).
  • Search, pages and posters fed by an online film database (September 2026).
  • With friends: finding other film lovers, friend requests and profiles (September 2026).
  • A four-step introduction, and a demo account to try the app without signing up (September 2026).
  • Artist pages with a progress gauge, works to tick off (Seen, To watch, Skipped) and four levels of appreciation (September 2026).
  • A golden badge when a filmography is complete (September 2026).
