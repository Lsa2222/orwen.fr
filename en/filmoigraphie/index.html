About the app

Filmoigraphie is an app for tracking and collecting filmographies, centred on actors and directors.

Film apps usually ask “Which films do you want to see?”. Filmoigraphie also asks: “Which actors and directors do you want to discover, follow and complete?”

  • A page for each artist, with a progress gauge (for example 34 works seen out of 52) and the chronological list of their works.
  • Tick off each work in one tap: Seen, To watch or Skipped.
  • A golden badge when a filmography is complete, that is when all its works are seen or skipped.
  • Four levels of appreciation, from “I hated it” to “I loved it”, for films, series, actors and directors, with a review and the viewing date.
  • From a film to its actors and directors, from an artist to their films: everything is linked.
  • With friends: find other film lovers, send friend requests, see profiles and completed filmographies, and for each film how many of your friends loved it.
  • The app itself is in French.
