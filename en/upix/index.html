About the app

Upix makes your photos sharper, cleaner and brighter in a single tap. Choose your photos, tap “Enhance”, and that’s it. Nothing to learn, no technical settings.

Ideal for photos that are small, dark, grainy or received by message, for pictures taken with an older phone, and for the ones you want to print big.

The enhancement runs entirely on your phone, even without Internet. Your photos are never sent anywhere, and there is no account to create.

  • Up to 50 photos at once, or share a photo to Upix from your gallery or another app
  • You can leave Upix while it works: a notification tells you when your photos are ready
  • Compare before and after with a slider, and pinch to zoom in on the details
  • Your enhanced photos go to the Upix album in your gallery, automatically or with the “Save” button, as you prefer
  • The date and camera details are kept, the location never is
  • Share the before / after side by side in a single image
  • Once you like the result, move the originals to the trash to avoid duplicates (always after your confirmation)
