Upix: progress

about 100%

Available

A rough percentage, deliberately cautious. As of November 16, 2026.

The app came out on Google Play on November 15, 2026: on-phone photo enhancement, natural-looking faces, batches that keep going in the background, six languages, ads and a purchase to remove them.

This project is on hold for now.

Latest changes

  • Faces stay natural, without a plastic look (October 2026).
  • Up to 50 photos at once, which keep going even if you leave the app, with a notification at the end (October 2026).
  • Sizes to choose from (social media, large screen, large print, custom), the before / after shared as one image, originals moved to the trash on request (October 2026).
  • Six languages: French, English, German, Spanish, Italian and Brazilian Portuguese (October 2026).
  • A “Report a problem” button on every photo (October 2026).
  • First complete version: choosing photos, on-phone enhancement, before / after comparison, saving and sharing (September 2026).
