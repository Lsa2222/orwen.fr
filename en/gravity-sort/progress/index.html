Gravity Sort

Gravity Sort: progress

about 70%

Final polish

A rough percentage, deliberately cautious. As of October 9, 2026.

The game is complete: 100 verified puzzles, a daily challenge, and a shop of themes and block styles. It has been tested on a phone; what remains is a last round of checks and getting it ready for release.

This project is on hold for now.

Latest changes

  • Daily wheel: one free spin a day, with the odds of each prize shown (October 2026).
  • Daily challenge: streak bonuses after 3, 7 and 14 days, then every week (October 2026).
  • Bigger shop: 9 themes and 8 block styles, which can be tried for two levels in exchange for an optional ad (October 2026).
  • All 100 levels remade to call for real thinking: repeating the same swipe without looking at the board no longer works, except in the introductory levels (September 2026).
  • Difficulty in waves: 14 hard levels flagged with a badge, better rewarded and followed by a breather (September 2026).
  • Star chest: coins and hints given as a reward for the stars earned (September 2026).

In progress

  • Touch-ups to the shop and the wheel following the phone tests.

Coming next

  • Final checks on a phone, including the display at the largest text size.
  • Publishing the privacy policy online.
  • Screenshots for the store page.
  • Final version, tried in a private test on a real phone before release.
