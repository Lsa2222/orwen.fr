About the game

Swipe to change gravity: every block slides at the same time until it hits a wall, a rock or another block. Group all the blocks of the same color to solve the puzzle!

Easy to learn in seconds, surprisingly deep to master.

  • 100 carefully tuned puzzles in 5 worlds, each one proven solvable
  • New mechanics along the way: walls, rocks, bolted anchor blocks, limited moves
  • Daily challenge: the same puzzle for everyone, beat your best moves and time
  • Undo and restart anytime, no timer in the main levels
  • Colorblind friendly: every color also has its own symbol
  • Playable offline, small download, smooth on older phones
