About the game

In this dungeon, down is never in the same place. Swipe a finger and gravity flips. The floor becomes a wall, the ceiling becomes the floor, and everything falls with you. Crates, bombs, traps… and monsters.

Gravity is your weapon. Drop a boulder on a skeleton. Send a knight falling onto spikes. Land with all your weight on a pack of monsters.

Between descents, the Sanctuary keeps your crystals: unlock new heroes, new upgrades and permanent bonuses.

  • 3 worlds: the Crypts, the Crystal Mines, the Observatory.
  • Monsters and 3 bosses that play with gravity too.
  • 40 upgrades to combine. Become heavy as an anvil, fall like a meteor, or float like a feather.
  • Three heroes: the Apprentice, well balanced; the Maul, slow and devastating; Feather, fragile and hard to catch.
  • Runs of 15 to 20 minutes. Playable offline.
  • Free. No forced ads. You choose to watch one for a bonus (get back up, double your reward…), or not.
