About the game

Turn the conveyor arrows, press START and watch your objects rush to the exit of their color. Easy to learn, increasingly clever to solve!

START is free: try as many times as you like. Stuck? A hint shows the tile to rotate and where to point it. Solve a level in few moves to earn all 3 stars.

Free, supported by ads. No real-money purchases. No account needed: your progress stays on your phone.

  • 60 brain-teasing levels: five worlds that bring in crossroads, locked arrows and glowing tiles step by step.
  • A skull marks the HARD and SUPER HARD levels on the map. They pay two to three times more coins.
  • A new puzzle every day and a streak to keep, a daily wheel and gift, and a chest for every 25 stars you earn.
  • 10 themes (Factory, Neon, Candy, Space, Nature, Ancient, Lava, Cyber, Ocean, Aurora) and 9 object styles to unlock with the coins you earn by playing.
  • An option adds one symbol per color (triangle, circle, square, diamond) so you can play even when colors look alike to you.
