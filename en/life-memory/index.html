About the app

Life Memory is a geographic life journal: little by little, you build a map of the places that have mattered in your life.

Each memory sits on the map with its title, location, date, category, a description and photos: a first trip to Japan, a concert, an important moment.

No account needed. Your photos stay on this device! They are personal, and they stay that way.

  • The map: start building your personal map by adding your first memory.
  • The timeline: your memories in chronological order.
  • Statistics: an overview of your life journey, in memories, countries, cities, years and categories.
  • Search for a city or a place to pin a memory.
  • Export your memories and photos to a ZIP archive, and restore them from it.
