Collectionception

Collectionception: progress

about 65%

Final polish

A rough percentage, deliberately cautious. As of October 9, 2026.

All the planned features are in place and the app installs on a phone. What remains is to check everything in real-world use, then to prepare the release.

This project is on hold for now.

Latest changes

  • After a reinstall, the app flags missing photos and offers to restore them from a backup (October 2026).
  • Sync: photos only travel over Wi-Fi, unless the setting is changed (October 2026).
  • Optional sync of collections and their photos across several phones, with no account to create (October 2026).
  • Sharing a collection, as a file or through a link, plus automatic backup by the phone (October 2026).
  • Two home screen widgets: collection progress and upcoming purchases (October 2026).
  • App rebuilt to work entirely offline, with a redesigned interface (October 2026).

In progress

  • Checking every screen on a real phone: display, animations, smoothness.
  • First real-world trials of sync and link sharing.
  • On-phone trials of photos, barcode scanning, the lock and the widgets.

Coming next

  • Preparing the release: the app's final identity, the store listing and the privacy policy.
  • Trying the final version in internal testing, before any release.
  • Idea kept for later: estimating the value of items automatically.
  • Idea kept for later: recognising an item from a photo.
