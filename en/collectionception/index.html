About the app

Collectionception is an Android app for keeping track of your collections, whether cards, manga, figurines, games, vinyl records or anything else: what you own, what is missing, what you want to buy and what it is all worth.

The app is in French, designed for phones, and works entirely offline: your data stays on the device. Optionally, syncing through your own Google Drive brings it to all your phones.

  • Collections and sub-collections, with a category, a cover image and custom fields.
  • Status in one tap: owned (with a quantity for duplicates), missing, wanted (with a level of desire), for sale, for trade.
  • Quick add: one entry after another, a numbered series, a pasted list, or a barcode (books are recognised automatically).
  • To buy: the list of everything you want, with a monthly budget, upcoming releases and a shop mode, a pared-down checklist to use in store.
  • Statistics: progress, value, gain in value, acquisitions month by month, items currently on loan.
  • Backup to a file (photos included), and export of a collection as a PDF, a CSV or an image to share.
  • Lock with your fingerprint, your face or the phone's code.
  • Two home screen widgets: the progress of your collections, and your next three purchases.
