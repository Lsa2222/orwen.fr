Council of Six Realms

Council of Six Realms: progress

about 70%

Final polish

A rough percentage, deliberately cautious. As of October 9, 2026.

The creative work is finished: 430 cards, 22 reign endings and 80 characters, in French and English, with sound and music in place. Every automated phone test has passed; what remains is the author's own final playtest, then the publishing steps.

This project is on hold for now.

Latest changes

  • Successful tests on a real phone: screens, saving, smoothness, daily reminders (October 2026).
  • The game's sound effects and music chosen by ear and put in place (October 2026).
  • Full review of the game: about twenty issues found, none serious, all fixed (October 2026).
  • Release materials ready: icon, banners, screenshots and store listing in both languages (October 2026).
  • Complete English translation: all 430 cards, 22 reign endings and 80 character profiles (October 2026).
  • Writing finished: 430 decision cards, 22 reign endings, 40 objectives (October 2026).

In progress

  • The author's own playtest of the full game on a phone: the sound, the music, the swipe and how good it feels to play are still to be judged.

Coming next

  • Putting the privacy policy online.
  • Release version 1.0.0.
  • Real-world test of purchases, achievements and the leaderboard.
  • A closed test with a small group of players.
  • Going live, once the closed test is over.
