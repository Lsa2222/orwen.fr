About the game

The gameplay loop: get monsters, build your team, play tower defense, receive rewards, fuse, discover new creatures, then start again.

Current content: 353 monsters spread across 7 ranks and 9 lineages, 54 enemies including 16 bosses, and 10 levels that make up the whole of World 1 (5 in the swamp, 5 in the corrupted woods).

  • Monster collection, inventory, team and discovery
  • Fusion by synthesis with 2 and 4 parents: lineages, choice of the result, inheritance
  • Tower defense: map, waves, enemies, combat
  • Rewards, monster levels, expeditions, recruitment, summoning
  • On Android, in portrait mode
