About the game

Welcome to Monsters Match, the match 3 puzzle full of funny monsters!

Slide monsters to line up 3 of the same colour. Match 4 to create a blaster, make an L or a T for a bomb, and 5 in a row for a crystal that clears a whole colour. Combine them for spectacular explosions!

Relax, take on the challenges and collect every monster!

  • 150 levels in 12 worlds
  • Monsters that evolve: let them grow and they count double, triple… up to 5 times!
  • Varied challenges: crates, ice, lava rocks, growing vines, dirt to clean, hearts to bring down
  • Album of 25 monsters to collect
  • Play offline, anywhere
  • Everything is earned by playing: no real-money purchases
