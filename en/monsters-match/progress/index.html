Monsters Match

Monsters Match: progress

about 70%

Final polish

A rough percentage, deliberately cautious. As of October 9, 2026.

The game is complete: 150 levels across 12 worlds, in 9 languages. In early October a full review fixed 30 bugs and tuned the difficulty level by level; getting it ready for release is what remains.

This project is on hold for now.

Latest changes

  • Difficulty raised a notch after the first playtests: one move fewer per level beyond the first world (October 2026).
  • Tested on a real phone: three flaws found, then fixed (October 2026).
  • Six new colour themes: Ocean, Forest, Lava, Ice, Aurora and Royal (October 2026).
  • All 150 levels tuned one by one: difficulty rises in waves, with 'hard' levels flagged on the map, each followed by a gentler one (October 2026).
  • 30 bugs fixed, including a win that could be lost if the game was closed during the end-of-level animation (October 2026).
  • Evolving monsters, which grow on the board up to a fifth stage, and the first colour themes (September 2026).

In progress

  • One decision is pending: whether to make the free boosters a little more generous now that the game is harder.
  • Trying the harder version on a phone.

Coming next

  • Last hands-on checks on a phone: sound, vibration, reminders, smoothness on a modest device.
  • Proofreading of the translations, ideally by native speakers.
  • New screenshots for the store page, since the themes and the hard levels now look different.
  • Publishing the privacy policy online.
  • Closed test with a small group of players before release.
