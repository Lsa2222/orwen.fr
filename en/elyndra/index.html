About the game

She saved the world. She destroyed three kingdoms. Both are true.

Elyndra: Echoes of Fate is a turn-based tactical RPG built for phones: short, readable grid battles, a story you piece together yourself, and characters whose bonds truly matter.

All over the kingdom, statues honour Saint Elyndra, who died sealing a demon. The oldest statues do not smile: they were remade.

  • Eight classes, sixteen promotions, skills that reshape the battlefield.
  • Sixteen heroes, thirty-four pairs. Let them fight side by side and their bond grows from rank C to rank S.
  • Twenty chapters, ten side stories, three endings.
  • Ten to twenty minute sessions, autosave, resume in the middle of a battle.
  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscription, no gacha.
  • Fully playable offline. The game collects no data.
