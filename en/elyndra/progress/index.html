Elyndra: Echoes of Fate

Elyndra: Echoes of Fate: progress

about 70%

Final polish

A rough percentage, deliberately cautious. As of October 9, 2026.

The twenty chapters and the ten side missions are written, with their maps, in French and in English. Work is now focused on the character artwork, with final polish to come.

This project is on hold for now.

Latest changes

  • New dialogue portraits for four heroes, each with six expressions (October 2026).
  • Two new faces among the sixteen heroes: Dariane, a forty-year-old knight, and Maël, a young healer (October 2026).
  • New look chosen for the characters on the battle maps: painted artwork seen from above will replace the 3D figures (October 2026).
  • Close-up duel scene on every attack, which can be tuned or switched off in the options (October 2026).
  • Twenty chapters, ten side missions and all the conversations between heroes written, then translated into English (October 2026).
  • All of the game's music is in place (October 2026).

In progress

  • Drawing every character in the new painted style: after the front view come the back, attack and walking views.
  • Final touches on Renn, the archer: his crossbow, his portrait and his promoted outfit.
  • Visual effects for the elements and the skills.

Coming next

  • Illustrated characters brought into the whole game in place of the 3D figures: battle maps, camp and world map.
  • Promoted outfits for the last heroes.
  • A full playthrough from start to finish, to fix and polish the details.
  • New screenshots for the store page.
